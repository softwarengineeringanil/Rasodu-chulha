Privacy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

What this website stores

This website does not store your order details. It has no accounts, no database and no payment system.

When you place an order

The name, mobile number, delivery address, landmark, preferred time and instructions you type into the order form are used only to write a WhatsApp message on your own device. They reach Rasodu Chulha only if you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp’s own privacy terms apply to that conversation, and the message stays in that WhatsApp chat.

Analytics

Analytics is switched off unless the site owner turns it on. If it is on, Google Analytics loads only after you choose “Allow”, and you can say “No thanks”. It counts things like visits, button clicks and the number of plates selected. It is never sent your name, phone number, address or notes. Your choice is remembered in your browser’s local storage.

Cookies and storage

The website sets no cookies of its own. If you allow analytics, Google Analytics may set its own cookies.

Other services this site uses

  • Google Fonts: your browser fetches the fonts from Google, which receives your IP address for that request.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): opens when you order or send feedback.
  • Instagram: the footer link takes you to Instagram, which has its own policy.
  • Google Analytics: only if enabled and allowed, as described above.

Reviews and delivery tracking

The site has no review form and no delivery tracking today, so it collects no review data and no rider or customer location. If either is added, this page will be updated first.
